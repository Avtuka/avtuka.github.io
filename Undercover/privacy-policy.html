🕵️ Undercover

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026

The short version

  • Undercover works entirely offline. Nothing you enter or capture in the app is sent anywhere.
  • Player names, optional player photos, word decks, and game progress are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party data-sharing SDKs.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local data.

1. Who this policy covers

Undercover ("the app", "we", "our") is a local, pass-and-play party game for Android, iOS, and other supported platforms. This policy explains what information the app accesses on your device, how it is used, and your choices — because the app does not have a server or backend, there is no "our systems" to describe beyond your own device.

2. Information the app accesses

Everything below is created by you during play and stored locally on your device. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party, because the app makes no network connections.

DataWhy it's collectedWhere it's stored
Player names Displayed on dossier cards and throughout the round so players can identify each other. Local app storage on your device only.
Player photos (optional) You may capture a photo with your device camera for a player's dossier card. This step is entirely optional — the game works fine without it. Saved to local device/app storage; never uploaded.
Word categories & word pairs The words used during play, including any custom decks you create. Local SQLite database on your device.
In-progress game state Lets you resume a game if the app is closed or the device restarts (round, roles, scores, etc.). Local device preferences storage; overwritten each time you progress, cleared when you end the game.

3. Device permissions we ask for

PermissionPurpose
Camera Only used if you choose to take a photo for a player's dossier card. The camera is never accessed without you tapping the photo button.
Vibration / Haptics Provides tactile feedback for in-game actions (e.g. hold-to-reveal). Purely cosmetic — no data involved.
Photo / storage access Needed on some older Android versions to save and reload the photo you captured for a dossier card.

You can grant or revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device's system settings. Denying camera access simply means you won't be able to add a player photo — every other feature keeps working.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, sign-in, or email address to use the app.
  • We don't collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • We don't show ads or use ad networks.
  • We don't use cookies, device identifiers, or any tracking technology.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data — there is no data on our end to share.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, or microphone.

5. Children's privacy

Undercover does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because no information ever leaves the device. Parents and guardians can review or delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app.

6. Data retention & deletion

Local data persists on your device only for as long as the app is installed. You can remove it at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app (removes all stored names, photos, decks, and game state), or
  • Using your device's app-storage/clear-data option, or
  • Deleting individual custom word decks or player photos from within the app.

7. Security

Because all data stays within your device's app-sandboxed storage, it is protected by your operating system's standard app-sandboxing and, where enabled, device-level encryption. As with any app, we recommend keeping your device's OS up to date and using a device passcode.

8. Changes to this policy

If Undercover's data practices change in the future (for example, if an optional online feature is added), this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
