Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my data?
None of our apps require an account, so there's no server-side data to delete. All app data lives on your device — uninstalling the app or clearing its storage from your device settings removes everything.
An ad won't load — is something wrong?
Ads are served by Google AdMob and depend on your internet connection and ad availability in your region. Try again in a few minutes, or check that you're connected to the internet.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Email us at avtukalaz@gmail.com with your device model, OS version, and the app version if possible, along with steps to reproduce the issue.
Can I request a new feature?
Yes — we're happy to hear suggestions. Send them to avtukalaz@gmail.com.
Do these apps work offline?
Undercover works fully offline. Mafia Anchor and Joker work offline for gameplay, but need an internet connection to show rewarded ads.
